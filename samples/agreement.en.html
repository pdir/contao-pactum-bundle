Data Processing Agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR

between

{{customer.company}}
{{customer.address}}
– hereinafter "Controller" –

and

{{contractor.name}}
{{contractor.address}}
represented by {{contractor.representative}}
– hereinafter "Processor" –

1. Subject matter and duration

(1) The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller within the scope of the service agreement between the parties (main agreement) regarding: [describe the service, e.g. hosting and maintenance of the website].

(2) The term of this agreement corresponds to the term of the main agreement. The obligations to delete or return data under section 9 remain unaffected.

2. Nature, purpose and scope of processing

(1) Nature and purpose of processing: [e.g. storage, backup and technical provision of website data].

(2) Types of personal data: [e.g. contact data, communication data, usage data, contract data].

(3) Categories of data subjects: [e.g. customers, prospects, website visitors, employees of the Controller].

(4) Processing generally takes place in a member state of the EU or the EEA. Processing in a third country only takes place under the conditions of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

3. Instructions

(1) The Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law (Art. 28(3)(a) GDPR).

(2) The Processor shall immediately inform the Controller if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes data protection provisions.

4. Confidentiality

The Processor ensures that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality (Art. 28(3)(b) GDPR).

5. Security of processing

The Processor takes all technical and organisational measures required pursuant to Art. 32 GDPR. The measures applicable at the conclusion of this agreement are described in the annex. Further development is permitted provided that the level of protection is not reduced.

{{annex.tom}}

6. Sub-processors

(1) The Controller gives general authorisation to engage further processors. The sub-processors engaged at the conclusion of this agreement are listed in the annex.

(2) The Processor informs the Controller in advance of any intended changes. The Controller may object within [14] days for an important reason relating to data protection.

(3) The Processor imposes the same data protection obligations as set out in this agreement on its sub-processors (Art. 28(4) GDPR).

{{annex.subprocessors}}

7. Assistance to the Controller

(1) The Processor assists the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in fulfilling the rights of data subjects (Art. 12 to 23 GDPR).

(2) Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to it, the Processor assists the Controller in ensuring compliance with the obligations pursuant to Art. 32 to 36 GDPR.

(3) The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

8. Evidence and audits

The Processor makes available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Art. 28 GDPR and allows for audits, including inspections, after reasonable prior notice (Art. 28(3)(h) GDPR).

9. Deletion and return

After the end of the provision of services, the Processor deletes or returns all personal data at the choice of the Controller, unless there is a legal obligation to store the data (Art. 28(3)(g) GDPR).

10. Data protection officer

Data protection contact of the Processor: {{contractor.dpo_name}}, {{contractor.dpo_contact}}.

11. Final provisions

(1) In the event of contradictions between this agreement and the main agreement, the provisions of this agreement take precedence insofar as the protection of personal data is concerned.

(2) Should individual provisions be invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions remains unaffected.

(3) The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies.
